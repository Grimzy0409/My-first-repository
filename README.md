# My Favorite Actor: Jackie Chan

## About Jackie Chan
**Jackie Chan** is a world-famous action movie star, known for his amazing martial arts, stunts, and comedy.  
He started his career in Hong Kong and later became an international icon in Hollywood.

### Popular Movies
1. Rush Hour
2. Police Story
3. Drunken Master
4. The Karate Kid
5. Shanghai Noon

### Why I Admire Him
- Incredible action and stunt work  
- Great sense of humor  
- Inspiring dedication and discipline  

> “Do not let circumstances control you. You change your circumstances.”
